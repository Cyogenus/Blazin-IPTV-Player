Which should you use?

Use M3U if your source gives you a playlist file or playlist URL. Use Xtream Codes if your source gives you a server address, username, and password. Use M3U Plus if your source gives you a structured playlist URL that includes extra category and media information.

Why BLAZIN supports both

Windows users often have different source types over time. Supporting M3U, M3U Plus, Xtream Codes, STB MAC, and Stalker Portal means one app can cover more legal workflows without forcing the user to switch players.
