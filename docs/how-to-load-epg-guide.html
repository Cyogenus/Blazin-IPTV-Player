Why EPG may not appear

An empty guide usually means the source did not provide EPG data, the data failed to download, the entries do not match the channels, or the provider has outdated guide information.

Why EPG matters for Windows IPTV users

Windows users often work with large playlists. EPG support helps make those lists easier to browse by showing what is playing now and what comes next when the data is available.
