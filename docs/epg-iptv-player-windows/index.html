Trial checklist

How to test EPG quality before deciding

  1. Load your own legal M3U, Xtream Codes, STB MAC, or Stalker Portal source.
  2. Open the Live TV area and check whether guide information appears.
  3. Compare the schedule against channels you actually watch.

Troubleshooting

What to check when guide data is missing or wrong

  • If no guide appears, the source may not provide compatible EPG.
  • If only some channels have listings, the source may have incomplete channel matching.
  • If times look wrong, check source guide timing and device time settings.
  • If logos are missing, the source may not include logo metadata.
  • If EPG works in one source type but not another, compare the data returned by each source.

FAQ

EPG and TV guide questions

Does BLAZIN IPTV Player provide EPG data?

No. BLAZIN does not provide guide data. It displays compatible EPG information when your own legal IPTV source supplies it.

Why is my EPG empty?

The source may not include guide data, the EPG may not match the channels, or the source may use data that is not compatible.

Can M3U and Xtream sources both have EPG?

They can, depending on the source. BLAZIN displays compatible EPG data when it is included or returned by the source.

Does BLAZIN adjust all guide timing automatically?

Guide timing depends on the source data and the app workflow. During the trial, test whether the schedule matches the channels you use most.
