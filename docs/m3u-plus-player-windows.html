M3U Plus Player for Windows FAQ

What is M3U Plus?

M3U Plus is a playlist-style source format that may include additional grouping and metadata compared with a simple M3U file.

Does every M3U Plus source include Movies and Series?

No. Availability depends on the legal source you provide.

Is M3U Plus the same as Xtream Codes?

They are related in many IPTV workflows, but Xtream Codes usually uses server, username, and password login fields instead of only a playlist URL.
