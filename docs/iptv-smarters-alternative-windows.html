BLAZIN IPTV Player for Windows

BLAZIN IPTV Player is a fast, compact Windows IPTV player for users who want to load their own legal M3U, M3U Plus, Xtream Codes, STB MAC, or Stalker Portal source in one clean desktop app.

BLAZIN is designed for Windows users who want fast playlist loading, EPG support, internal VLC-based playback, external media player support, Movies and Series browsing when supported, themes, and a layout that does not take over the whole desktop.

Download from Microsoft Store View Screenshots

BLAZIN IPTV Player does not provide channels, playlists, accounts, subscriptions, or copyrighted content. Users must provide their own legal IPTV source.

Features

One Windows IPTV player for multiple source types

Instead of using separate players for different playlist formats, BLAZIN IPTV Player brings the most common IPTV login and playlist types into one Windows desktop app.

M3U & M3U Plus Player

Load M3U playlist files, M3U playlist URLs, and compatible M3U Plus sources. Browse large playlists in a clean Windows interface.

Xtream Codes Player

Use server, username, and password login details for compatible Xtream Codes sources with Live TV, Movies, and Series when available.

STB MAC & Stalker Portal

Support for compatible STB MAC and Stalker Portal sources, including advanced options for portals that require custom values.

EPG Guide Support

View electronic program guide data when your IPTV source provides EPG information for Live TV channels.

Internal VLC Playback

Play streams with the built-in VLC-based internal media player or route playback to your preferred external media player.

Themes and Skins

Switch between visual themes and skins to match your Windows desktop style.

How to Use

How to use BLAZIN IPTV Player on Windows

BLAZIN IPTV Player is made for users who already have their own legal IPTV source. After installing the app, choose the login type that matches your playlist or provider format.

  1. Install the app from the Microsoft Store.
    Download the official Windows version of BLAZIN IPTV Player from the Microsoft Store.
  2. Choose your source type.
    Select M3U, M3U Plus, Xtream Codes, STB MAC, or Stalker Portal depending on the details you have.
  3. Enter your playlist or login information.
    Add your M3U URL, M3U file, Xtream server credentials, STB MAC portal, or Stalker Portal details.
  4. Load your playlist.
    Browse Live TV, Movies, Series, categories, and EPG information when your source supports them.
  5. Start playback.
    Use the internal VLC-based player or configure an external media player for your preferred playback setup.

Best use cases

  • Windows users who need an IPTV player for M3U playlists.
  • Users who want Xtream Codes login support on Windows.
  • Users looking for STB MAC or Stalker Portal support.
  • Users who want EPG guide support in a compact desktop player.
  • Laptop and Microsoft Surface users who prefer a smaller app layout.
