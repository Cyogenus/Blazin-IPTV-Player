# Phase 5 Long-Tail Cleanup

This phase cleaned the older `.html` long-tail pages so they support the rebuilt BLAZIN IPTV Player site instead of acting like thin placeholder pages.

## Strategy

- Keep the canonical folder pages as the main product, source, and comparison pages.
- Rebuild useful long-tail pages that target real Windows IPTV workflows.
- Remove weak, duplicate, or unverified pages from the sitemap.
- Add `noindex, follow` to pages that should not be submitted to search engines until they are rebuilt.
- Keep every Microsoft Store CTA on `cid=Blazin_website`.
- Keep the legal boundary clear: BLAZIN IPTV Player does not provide channels, playlists, subscriptions, provider accounts, streams, or copyrighted content.

## Rebuilt indexable long-tail groups

- PC/laptop workflow pages
- Download/trial/Microsoft Store pages
- M3U, M3U8, M3U Plus helper pages
- EPG and Xtream Codes helper pages
- External player and themes pages
- Legal/no-subscription pages
- STB MAC vs Stalker Portal explainer
